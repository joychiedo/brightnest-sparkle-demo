# BrightNest Demo

Build a polished, production-quality cleaning company website redesign demo based directly on this Upwork brief:

“Wix Website Redesign For My Cleaning Website”

The purpose of this project is to create a convincing redesign concept for an existing local cleaning business website. The client wants a modern, clean, professional website that builds trust and makes it easier for visitors to request a quote or book a cleaning.

This is a portfolio/proposal demo, not a real client website. Use a fictional cleaning company name and fictional business information. Do not copy any real company's branding.

CORE GOAL

Create a website that demonstrates that I understand exactly what a professional cleaning-company website needs:

Strong homepage structure

Clear visual hierarchy

Modern professional design

Easy navigation

Mobile/tablet/desktop responsiveness

Clear “Get a Quote” and “Book a Cleaning” CTAs

Strong presentation of cleaning services

Professional imagery

Trust-building content

Testimonials/reviews

Service areas

Guarantee/trust section

Quote/contact functionality

SEO-friendly page structure

Polished local-business experience

The result should feel like a website a professional web designer/developer would confidently present to a real cleaning company.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

Use a quiet, premium, trustworthy local-business aesthetic.

Do NOT make it look like an AI-generated SaaS landing page.

Avoid:

gradients

excessive animations

glowing effects

glassmorphism

3D elements

excessive rounded cards

excessive shadows

giant text everywhere

fake futuristic graphics

unnecessary decorative elements

stock-template-looking layouts

Use:

strong typography

generous but controlled whitespace

clear spacing rhythm

restrained borders

subtle shadows only where useful

high-quality cleaning/home imagery

strong photography

professional contrast

clear CTAs

editorial-style composition

excellent responsive behavior

The design should communicate:

clean, reliable, professional, trustworthy, local, established.

BRAND

Create a fictional company:

BrightNest Cleaning Co.

Tagline:

A cleaner home. A better everyday.

Use a restrained professional palette built around:

warm white/off-white

deep charcoal

one restrained fresh accent color associated with cleanliness

Do not use a gradient.

Use a professional sans-serif font pairing.

NAVIGATION

Create a clean responsive navigation:

Home

Services

About

Service Areas

Reviews

Contact

Primary CTA:

Get a Free Quote

On mobile, use a clean responsive menu.

The navigation should remain simple and easy to understand.

HOMEPAGE

Build the homepage as a carefully structured conversion page.

1. HERO

Create a strong hero section with a professional image of a clean, bright home being professionally cleaned.

Headline:

Professional Cleaning. Done Right.

Supporting copy:

Reliable residential and commercial cleaning services designed around your home, your schedule, and your standards.

Primary CTA:

Get a Free Quote

Secondary CTA:

View Our Services

Include a small trust statement such as:

Trusted local cleaning professionals

Do not overcrowd the hero.

The first screen should immediately communicate:

What the company does

Who it serves

Why someone should trust it

What the visitor should do next

2. TRUST STRIP

Immediately below the hero, add a restrained trust section with items such as:

Reliable & Professional

Flexible Scheduling

Satisfaction Guaranteed

Fully Insured

Keep this visually clean rather than turning it into four huge cards.

3. SERVICES

Heading:

Cleaning Services That Fit Your Life

Supporting text explaining that customers can choose the cleaning service that fits their needs.

Create service offerings such as:

Regular Cleaning
Recurring cleaning for homes that need consistent upkeep.

Deep Cleaning
A detailed top-to-bottom clean for spaces that need extra attention.

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
Detailed cleaning before moving into or leaving a property.

Commercial Cleaning
Professional cleaning solutions for offices and small businesses.

Each service should have:

image

title

short description

subtle “Learn More” link

Do not make these generic SaaS cards. They should feel like real local-service offerings.

4. WHY CHOOSE US

Create a strong visual section explaining why customers should choose BrightNest.

Heading:

A Cleaner Space. Less To Worry About.

Include benefits such as:

Reliable scheduling

Detailed cleaning standards

Friendly professionals

Easy booking

Consistent service

Satisfaction guarantee

Use a large supporting image alongside the content.

5. HOW IT WORKS

Create a simple 3-step process:

1. Request a Quote
Tell us about your space and what you need.

2. Choose Your Service
Pick the cleaning service and schedule that works for you.

3. Enjoy a Cleaner Space
Our team handles the cleaning while you get on with your day.

Keep this section visually simple.

6. SOCIAL PROOF

Create a testimonial section.

Heading:

What Our Customers Say

Use 3 realistic fictional testimonials.

Example structure:

★★★★★

“BrightNest has made keeping our home clean so much easier. The team is reliable, professional, and always thorough.”

— Sarah M., Homeowner

Use believable names and locations, but clearly fictional/demo content.

7. SERVICE AREAS

Create a local-business service area section.

Heading:

Proudly Serving Local Homes & Businesses

Include fictional service areas such as:

Downtown

Westside

North Hills

Brookfield

Lakeside

Eastwood

Add a simple location visual or clean map-style treatment if appropriate.

Do not use a real Google Maps integration.

8. GUARANTEE / TRUST SECTION

Create a strong section around:

Your Satisfaction Matters

Copy should explain that the company stands behind its work and wants customers to feel confident booking.

Include:

Satisfaction Guaranteed

and a CTA:

Get Your Free Quote

9. FINAL CTA

Create a visually strong but restrained final conversion section.

Headline:

Ready for a Cleaner Space?

Supporting text:

Tell us what you need cleaned and we'll help you find the right service for your space.

Buttons:

Get a Free Quote

Book a Cleaning

10. FOOTER

Include:

BrightNest Cleaning Co.

short company description

navigation

services

service areas

contact information

business hours

social links

copyright

Use fictional information.

The user should be able to click:

Book a Cleaning

and reach a clean booking/request interface.

The objective is to demonstrate the UX the client asked for.

SEO STRUCTURE

Make the site SEO-friendly at the structural level.

Use:

one clear H1 per page

logical H2/H3 hierarchy

descriptive page titles

descriptive meta descriptions

semantic HTML

descriptive image alt text

clear service-specific copy

local service-area language

clean URLs/routes

Homepage title example:

BrightNest Cleaning Co. | Professional Home & Commercial Cleaning

Homepage meta description:

Professional residential and commercial cleaning services with flexible scheduling, reliable service, and a satisfaction guarantee. Request your free quote today.

Do not keyword-stuff.

RESPONSIVENESS

This is extremely important.

The website must be carefully designed for:

desktop

tablet

mobile

Do not simply shrink the desktop design.

On mobile:

navigation becomes a proper mobile menu

hero typography scales appropriately

buttons remain easy to tap

forms become single-column

images resize/crop intelligently

sections maintain comfortable spacing

no horizontal overflow

testimonials remain readable

CTA sections remain prominent

Test the entire site at common mobile widths.

WIX / WIX STUDIO MENTAL MODEL

Although this demo may be implemented using the available development stack, design and structure it as if it were being delivered through Wix Studio.

The demo should demonstrate understanding of what a Wix client expects:

visual section-based page building

responsive breakpoints

reusable content structures

CMS-friendly service/testimonial content

forms

booking/quote flows

SEO configuration

local-business content

easy future content updates

Do not build unnecessary custom engineering that would make the solution harder to reproduce in Wix.

The architecture and UX should be realistic to recreate in Wix Studio.

TECHNICAL QUALITY

If using React/Next.js:

componentize repeated sections

use clean reusable components

keep the code organized

avoid unnecessary dependencies

make all interactions functional

use responsive CSS/Tailwind

optimize images

ensure accessibility

use semantic elements

include accessible labels for forms

ensure keyboard navigation works

provide visible focus states

avoid console errors

The result must feel like a real professional website, not a coding exercise.

DEMO POLISH

Before finishing, inspect the entire website as though a potential Upwork client will see it for the first time.

Check:

Does the homepage immediately explain the business?

Is the CTA obvious?

Does the website look trustworthy?

Does it look like a real cleaning company?

Is the service information easy to scan?

Does the design feel professional?

Does mobile look intentionally designed?

Does the quote flow make sense?

Are there any awkward spacing issues?

Are there any placeholder-looking elements?

Are there any broken links?

Are all buttons functional?

Are forms validated?

Is the typography consistent?

Are images properly cropped?

Does the site feel like a redesign someone could actually present to a client?

Do a final visual QA pass and fix anything that looks unfinished.

The finished demo should be polished enough that I can show it to the Upwork client and say:
